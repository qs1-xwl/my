C1951 Monsters Pack
====================
Source: C1951.zip (from user, character/fx sprite sheet)

Roles (64x64 transparent PNG):
- Wolf  : 灰狼 - Idle 2 / Walk 6 / Attack 9 / Hit 1 / Dead 7 / Sing 10 (total 35)
- Evil  : 红色恶魔 - Idle 2 / Attack 9 / Hit 1 / Dead 7 (total 19)

Effects:
- Effects\Fire : 火焰特效 66 帧 (frame0..65), 可作怪物攻击特效

File naming: 每动作目录内 frame0..N.png, 按播放顺序命名.
Actions mapping: Sattack -> Attack (攻击), Sing -> Sing (施法/吼叫).

Use: 拖入项目后建 Animator 动画, Attack 用于 Enemy 攻击, Hit 受击, Dead 死亡.
